Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

Is MyFaithWalk free?
Yes — the app is free to download, and the core of your daily walk is free forever: daily devotionals, the prayer tracker, favorite verses, and journaling. An optional Premium subscription unlocks the full experience.
What does Premium include?
Premium unlocks unlimited journal entries, the Faith Chat companion, Rest & Renewal with guided breathing and ambient sounds, devotionals read aloud with natural voices, your full devotional calendar history — and every new feature we add in the future.
What languages does the app support?
MyFaithWalk is fully available in English and Spanish, including the daily devotionals.
Is my journal private?
Yes. Your journal entries and prayer requests are stored privately on your device — they are yours alone.
How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are handled by Apple and Google, so you can manage or cancel anytime in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings — no need to contact us.
How can I get help or share feedback?
We'd love to hear from you. Email us at faithwalkapp@gmail.com — we typically respond within 24 hours.
